# AI Usage Disclosure

Artificial Intelligence (AI) was used as a supporting tool during the development of this project.

## How AI Was Used

AI assistance was used for:

- Explaining Cyber Threat Intelligence (CTI) concepts and terminology.
- Helping structure the project and organize the weekly tasks.
- Assisting with the documentation and formatting of Markdown files.
- Explaining MITRE ATT&CK techniques and their relationship to the LockBit case study.
- Helping formulate the threat hunting hypothesis.
- Assisting with the construction and explanation of KQL queries used in Kibana.
- Reviewing the wording of the analysis and conclusions.
- Providing troubleshooting guidance during the ELK and Docker setup.

## What Was Performed by the Student

The following practical activities were performed by the student:

- Created and managed the GitHub project repository.
- Collected and analyzed threat intelligence using the selected OSINT tools.
- Worked with MISP during the CTI processing stage.
- Performed the Cyber Kill Chain and MITRE ATT&CK analysis.
- Installed and configured the ELK environment using Docker.
- Created the laboratory threat hunting dataset.
- Executed the KQL queries in Kibana.
- Reviewed the resulting PowerShell events.
- Analyzed the detected suspicious activity.
- Documented the results and prepared the final project.

## AI Role and Academic Integrity

AI was used as an assistance and learning tool, not as a replacement for the student's practical work.

Technical results presented in the project were obtained from the tools and laboratory environment used during the project. AI-generated explanations and documentation were reviewed and adapted by the student.

The student is responsible for the final content, technical implementation, analysis, and conclusions presented in this project.
