# AI Usage Disclosure — Week 10

## 1. Purpose of AI Assistance

AI tools were used as supporting resources during the APT29 threat intelligence case study.

The purpose was to help understand APT29, organize information from public threat intelligence reports, identify relevant MITRE ATT&CK techniques, and prepare the documentation.

## 2. Areas Where AI Was Used

AI assistance was used for:

- Explaining APT29 and its cyber espionage activities.
- Summarizing publicly available Mandiant research.
- Understanding the 2022 APT29 phishing campaign.
- Identifying relevant tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs).
- Mapping observed behaviors to MITRE ATT&CK.
- Organizing the attack lifecycle.
- Developing defensive recommendations.
- Formatting the GitHub report.

## 3. Student Contributions

The student reviewed the selected threat intelligence materials, studied the APT29 campaign, examined the MITRE ATT&CK framework, reviewed the proposed technique mappings, and prepared the final documentation.

## 4. Limitations

The report is based on publicly available threat intelligence and documented campaign behavior.

It is a case study and does not represent an independent forensic investigation or a practical simulation of an APT29 attack.

Some techniques are documented as part of APT29's broader activity and are not necessarily confirmed in every stage of the selected 2022 campaign.

## 5. Conclusion

AI tools supported research, technical explanations, and documentation. The student remained responsible for reviewing the information, understanding the techniques, and preparing the final submission.
